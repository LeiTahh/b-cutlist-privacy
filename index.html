Privacy Policy

Cutlist (the "App") — com.woodcutlist.app
Last updated: 2026-11-02 | Version: V1.0
In one sentence: this App collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting. Your projects and cut lists stay on your device. The only network traffic is Google Play billing, and only when you buy or restore Pro.

This is not "not implemented yet". It is the product: the App is free to download and earns money only from an optional one-time Pro purchase for data packs. Revenue does not depend on monetising data, so there is nothing to collect.

2. Your cut lists stay on your device

  • Projects are saved as plain JSON files inside the App's private directory: parts, board sizes, kerf source, grain direction, and the last project you opened. They are removed when you uninstall the App.
  • The nesting engine and the diagram run entirely on your phone. Sheet layouts, offcuts and warnings are computed locally; no server is asked to pack anything, and no project is ever uploaded for "analysis".
  • Built-in data (sheet sizes, material densities, nominal sizes) ships inside the install package as plain assets. It is data you can read, not a service you have to reach — the App works with the network switched off.

3. The only network traffic: Google Play billing

The App makes no HTTP requests of its own — no cloud sync, no analytics ping, no update check. Network access exists only because Google Play's billing library needs it when you tap Buy or Restore:

Who talks to the networkWhat happens
Google Play Billing (library code) Talks to Google Play to start or restore the purchase. The App receives back only whether the purchase is active, plus a purchase token.
The App's own Dart code No requests. There is no HTTP client in the application code.

The billing library bundles Google's datatransport component, which is why INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE appear in the merged manifest. The App never initialises that component, so there is no telemetry path in practice — but the permission is genuinely present in the package, which is why it is listed below instead of being hidden.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children and collects no one's personal information (see §1), so no special handling of children's data applies.

6. Data deletion

Because the App collects and stores nothing on any server, there is no "your data" to delete on our side.

  • Projects, settings and the Pro entitlement cache live in the App's private directory and are fully removed when you uninstall.
  • To clear them without uninstalling: Android Settings → Apps → Cutlist → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your saved projects, so export anything you want to keep first.
  • A purchase itself is tied to your Google account, not to this device. Clearing app data does not delete the purchase; use Restore in the App's settings to bring it back.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version ever adds collection behaviour (for example cloud backup, which would necessarily transfer your projects), this policy will be updated before the change takes effect and highlighted in-app.

The App's existing commitments do not change: no ads, no watermark, no time limit, and nothing you type or design leaves your device.
